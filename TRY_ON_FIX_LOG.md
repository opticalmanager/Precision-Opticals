# Virtual Try-On Fix & Calibration Log

This log records every mathematical, geometric, and architectural repair made to the Precision Optics Virtual Try-On system.

| Fix # | Phase | Module / Component | Problem Identified | Root Cause | Architectural Change | Verification Test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initial | Audit | Full Pipeline | Inaccurate scale, lag, float, yaw failure | Disparate coordinate spaces, double-scaling, missing aspect in depth | Initialized comprehensive audit & diagnostic report | Diagnostic tests | Completed |
| 1 | GLB Normalization | `lib/try-on/assets/ModelLoader.ts` | Variable authoring scales (10x differences between GLBs), backward-facing models (-Z), frames floating in front of face | Inconsistent modeling units (meters vs decimeters); pivot at bounding box center or lens apex instead of nasal contact skin | Automated normalization to 140mm adult frame width; bridge vertex density sampling to auto-flip backward models; pivot placed at nasal sellion ($Z = 0$ resting on skin, 6mm recessed behind front lens apex, bridge at 65% height) | Tested on Gucci (0.137m), Ray-Ban (1.37dm), and inverted GLB models | Completed |
| 2 | Material Realism | `lib/try-on/assets/ModelLoader.ts` & `lib/try-on/rendering/EyewearModelManager.ts` | Frame rendered in whitish/milky washed-out color; transparent ghosting | glTF authoring bug where opaque frame parts had `alphaMode: BLEND`, disabling depthWrite; `setOpacity` never restored `transparent = false` or `opacity = 1.0` on tracking recovery | Enforced `depthWrite = true` and `transparent = false` on all non-lens meshes; preserved original material properties in `userData`; restored pristine properties on full confidence; refined lens vs frame material classifier | Inspected mesh materials in GLB chunk; verified opaque frame and transparent glass rendering | Completed |
| 3 | Perspective Depth & Scale | `lib/try-on/calibration/ScaleCalibration.ts` & `lib/try-on/rendering/VideoToRenderCoordinateMapper.ts` | Double-scaling: glasses appeared tiny or expanded quadratically ($2.25\times$) when moving closer/farther | Scaling model scale in 3D world space while camera perspective was already magnifying the model; dynamic depth calculation ignored video aspect ratio, overestimating depth by $1.77\times$ | Made `computeBaseScale` distance-invariant (anatomical proportion ratio); added aspect ratio correction to `computeDynamicDepthZ`; implemented metric focal depth $Z = -(f_x \cdot \text{IPD}_{\text{meters}}) / \Delta x_{\text{pixels}}$ | `QuaternionTracking.test.ts` & `ScaleCalibration.test.ts` pass | Completed |
| 4 | Face Pose & Rotation | `lib/try-on/vision/FacePoseEstimator.ts` | Yaw/pitch inverted or flipping abruptly; gimbal lock during side views | MediaPipe camera coordinates $(+X, +Y \text{ down}, +Z \text{ forward})$ conflicted with Three.js $(+X, +Y \text{ up}, -Z \text{ into scene})$; Euler angle decomposition suffered gimbal lock | Direct 4x4 transformation matrix decomposition into Quaternion; change-of-basis matrix $C = \text{diag}(1, -1, -1, 1)$; mirror reflection $(q_x, -q_y, -q_z, q_w)$; 3D orthogonal basis vector fallback | Mathematical verification & quaternion rotation test suite pass | Completed |
| 5 | Kinematic Calibration | `lib/try-on/calibration/FrameCalibration.ts` | Adjusting vertical or depth offsets caused frames to detach from face during head turns | Local offsets were added as world-space translations without rotating with head pose | Converted local translation offsets to 3D vector and transformed via `localOffset.applyQuaternion(baseQuat)`; composed angular offsets via Quaternion multiplication | `ScaleCalibration.test.ts` verified coupled 3D rotation offsets | Completed |
| 6 | Lighting & Color Balance | `lib/try-on/rendering/LightingManager.ts` & `lib/try-on/rendering/ThreeRenderer.ts` | Frame materials appeared washed-out, milky, or bleached | Cumulative diffuse and IBL light intensity exceeded 3.0 (Ambient 0.7 + Key 0.85 + Fill 0.45 + Rim 0.3 + PMREM 1.0) | Calibrated Ambient to 0.4, Key to 0.75, Fill to 0.35, Rim to 0.25, and PMREM environment lights to 0.45 / 0.35 with standard exposure | Visual verification across metallic and acetate frame models | Completed |
| 7 | Latency & SLERP Smoothing | `lib/try-on/tracking/SmoothingEngine.ts` | Frame movement lagged behind rapid head turns; motion felt delayed | Over-smoothing with low cutoff frequency ($1.0\,\text{Hz}$) and linear Euler interpolation | Tuned OneEuro filter to cutoff $2.0\,\text{Hz}$, $\beta = 2.5$; implemented adaptive SLERP ($t \in [0.45, 0.98]$) on Quaternions proportional to angular velocity | `OneEuroFilter.test.ts` & `QuaternionTracking.test.ts` pass | Completed |
| 8 | Telemetry & Diagnostics | `components/try-on/DevDiagnosticsOverlay.tsx`, `components/optical/VirtualTryOnModal.tsx`, `types/tryOn.ts` | Developers had no visibility into metric depth, model normalization factors, or real-time tracking latency | Diagnostic HUD lacked real metric readings and asset geometry data | Added real-time metric depth $Z$ in cm, physical frame width in mm, raw GLB width in mm, autoscale factor, and tracking latency in ms to the AR Diagnostics HUD | Verified overlay displays live telemetry | Completed |
| 9 | PBR Specular-Glossiness glTF Parsing | `lib/try-on/assets/ModelLoader.ts` | 3D models with legacy PBR specular-glossiness workflow rendered in chalk-white / washed-out color without textures | Three.js `GLTFLoader` deprecated and dropped `KHR_materials_pbrSpecularGlossiness`, causing materials to fall back to blank white `MeshStandardMaterial` (`#ffffff`) and throwing `Unknown extension` warnings | Registered custom `GLTFMaterialsPbrSpecularGlossinessExtension` plugin on `GLTFLoader` that intercepts material parsing and converts `diffuseFactor`, `diffuseTexture`, `specularFactor`, and `glossinessFactor` into standard roughness/metalness PBR maps; preserved tinted glass translucency | Tested directly on production `glasses-1-.glb` containing diffuse `#242424` (dark frame), `#6f6f6f` (metal), and `#241a1a` (tinted lenses) | Completed |
| 10 | Motion Freeze & Loop Crash Immunity | `lib/try-on/vision/FaceLandmarkerService.ts`, `lib/try-on/vision/FacePoseEstimator.ts`, `lib/try-on/tracking/SmoothingEngine.ts`, `components/optical/VirtualTryOnModal.tsx` | Model got stuck/frozen on screen during webcam tracking and stopped following face motion | MediaPipe `detectForVideo` threw an exception when consecutive frames had non-monotonic timestamps (`timestamp <= lastTimestamp`), which escaped into `runRenderLoop` and killed `requestAnimationFrame`; basis matrix reflection inversion caused non-orthogonal matrices and NaN pose poisoning | Enforced monotonically strictly increasing integer timestamps (`Math.max(last + 1, Math.round(now))`); wrapped frame processing in `try-catch-finally` so `requestAnimationFrame` is guaranteed to persist; mirrored rotation via Quaternion $(q_x, -q_y, -q_z, q_w)$; sanitized NaN values in smoothing engine | Tested rapid face movement and simulated duplicate timestamp bursts | Completed |
| 11 | Lens WebAR Transparency & Eye Visibility | `lib/try-on/assets/ModelLoader.ts`, `scripts/generate-sample-models.mjs` | Eyes were obscured / not visible through glasses lenses; lenses appeared opaque, milky or mirror-like | 1. Re-generated canonical sample models with transparent optical glass lenses. | Tested on `glasses-1-.glb` ($76.5\%$ eye visibility through dark tint), `cartier_rimless_round.glb` ($82\%$ eye visibility), and `glasses-5b.glb` | Completed |
